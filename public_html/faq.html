Frequently Asked Questions

What is this site for?

It’s a course StudyBot for the CUHK LAW course Hong Kong Constitutional Law. It explains lecture content, suggests examples, and answers follow-up questions. By combining course-specific retrieval with general legal reasoning in a way that reduces the chance of hallucinations, Prof. Hargreaves hopes to facilitate independent study and the exploration of legal concepts and ideas related to constitutional law generally. It is only a supplement to the course, not a replacement.

Where do the answers come from?

Primarily from the course material you’ve been given, but the bot may sometimes also draw from the training database of the underlying AI model (DeepSeek) to expand on answers or give fuller explanations. If there’s any conflict between the course material and the underlying training data, the bot is instructed to allow the course material to prevail.

Will the bot always be right?

No. It may make mistakes. Generative AI tools are still subject to hallucinations and other kinds of errors. If you're not sure about the accuracy of an answer, check with your classmates or the professor. If you encounter serious errors, let Prof. Hargreaves know.

Is my usage recorded?

Yes. Questions and answers are anonymously logged for course improvement and evaluation. No IP addresses or browser details are stored with your questions.

How do I get a copy of my chat?

Open the ⋯ menu at the top of the chat page and choose Copy transcript or Email transcript after you start a conversation.

Why did you create this site?

I'm interested in experimenting with all kinds of AI tools in legal education. If you're interested too, feel free to contact me to discuss.

How was this StudyBot built?

  • Custom interface: A dedicated web front-end designed to make the chat experience simple and accessible on desktop and mobile, built with the help of AI coding tools.
  • Document processing: Lecture slides and copyright-free material such as the Basic Law, PRC Constitution, and the Bill of Rights Ordinance are uploaded and converted into a searchable index using open-source parsers.
  • Retrieval-augmented generation: When you ask a question, the StudyBot fetches the most relevant slide excerpts and provides them to the language model as context before answering.
  • AI engine: Responses are generated by DeepSeek's API, with a carefully designed system prompt to keep answers concise, accurate, and aligned with the course.
  • Hosting and services: The site runs on PHP with Cloudflare Workers handling secure calls to the DeepSeek API.
  • Logging and review: Anonymised logs are stored for research into how students use the StudyBot and how it might be improved.
  • Vibes & Coffee: Self-explanatory!
